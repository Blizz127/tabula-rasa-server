## Server, mission and NPC evidence

The clearest early-progression sequence is in [Walkabout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1WM5ddg-k); the player's level-5 indicator is visible. At [24:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1WM5ddg-k&t=1488s), the player is speaking to **Warrior Apirka** and the “Conscientious Objector” completion panel offers a choice of two visible items plus a numeric reward. At [24:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1WM5ddg-k&t=1492s), the mission completes and the client displays **+2,000 XP**. At [24:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1WM5ddg-k&t=1495s), Apirka immediately offers “Conscientious Objector - Part Two” with the objective **“Bring the report to Rogers.”** The player accepts it at [25:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-I1WM5ddg-k&t=1510s). This corroborates the current seed's Apirka giver and Rogers receiver, and the separately seeded 2,000 XP for mission 1390. The choice item templates remain unidentified; neither the 1392 nor 1393 branch ID can be assigned from the visible title alone. The recording date and client build are unknown, so this is supporting footage rather than proof of shutdown-day rewards.

In the [Pravus run at 00:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4udsM0rcLo&t=30s), the tracker shows “Wilderness Targets of Opportunity” with **Kill 40 Miasmas**, **Kill 40 Xanx** and **Kill 30 Shield Drones** counters. These match objectives 7, 3 and 4 of client mission 1449 in `MissionClientObjectiveSkeletonRows.cs`. The same video shows the zone labels **Frontlines** at 00:30, **Pravus Interior Halls** at [05:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4udsM0rcLo&t=300s), and **Pravus Production Chamber** at [07:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4udsM0rcLo&t=420s), along with Thrax Riflemen/Technicians and the Prototype Forean Machina target. These are encounter and map leads; spawn counts, server timers, drops and final-live mission availability remain unverified. The player's visible x2 XP indicator and group bonus also make this clip unsuitable for calibrating ordinary XP rates.

The remaining reviewed event and combat footage supplies limited later-segment leads: [Brick Dance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7erRjukj90c) shows a player fighting a Kael called Brick outside Fort Dew, but its uploader explicitly says the character is level 30 against level 24 enemies; [Spy Style](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7Wr-HEzPFEM) is an edited Spy combat montage; [Olympics Day 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pLl2i-iJSWU) depicts player-organized shooting and other competitions. None sets normal encounter difficulty, class damage or a persistent event schedule. These belong after the current boot camp and Wilderness progression segments.

## Neuronet use

The strongest bot-relevant observation is **situational group behavior**: visible players remain with their squad while moving from outdoor fights into indoor combat in Pravus. Mira Vey and Tavin Rook already follow Blizz and may assist a target through native combat; [the current operator record](../../../dev/dit-human-behavior-platform-DIT-Neuronetwork/docs/operators/TABULA_RASA_TWO_BOT_TEST.md) says autonomous quest actions, learned route export, human motion scorecards, layered stuck recovery and event chatter are still incomplete. The footage supplies scenarios for later comparison, not numeric movement or combat tuning.

For future behavior validation, compare a continuous Pravus sequence against bot telemetry for (1) squad continuity through entrances and turns, (2) pauses and approach during combat, and (3) recovery after the leader moves away. Record measured spacing and time uncertainty before changing a follower parameter. The older social clips can inform event-specific conversation or gestures only if the corresponding original emote/event exists on the final live client and the bot actually has that action. Do not make bots enact a blind race, a PTS exercise, or an obsolete XP rate as normal behavior.

Four narrowly worded historical facts were sent to Neuronet's live `Learning/Teach` API: the Pravus squad observation, the D11 **PTS** exercise boundary, the 2008 player event context, and the Apirka/Part Two sequence. All four returned `verified` and were read back from its learning view. Their exact text, source links and returned task IDs are in the inventory. The facts explicitly state their date/build limits. `TeachFact` still stores them as verified operator references, so they are suitable only as dated conversational context; they do not drive bot movement, combat or quests. No live DIT files or game container were changed.
